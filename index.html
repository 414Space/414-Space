ABOUT
THE ARTIST

Jinxuan
[ARTIST PHOTO — upload images/artist.jpg]

Using mixed media, focusing on self-expression and the connection between people and nature.

Jinxuan Jiang is an animator and painter exploring themes of memory, time, nature, and the self, with a particular interest in the spaces in between. Working across animation, drawing, moving image, and writing,I enjoy exploring narrative structures that weave together dreams, memories, and lived experience. My work often begins with fragments—a memory, an image, or a fleeting sensation. Rather than starting with a fixed framework, I develop ideas through making, allowing the work to evolve organically as the process unfolds. My creative practice is an ongoing exploration of personal modes of expression. I aim to use animation as a medium to articulate experiences and feelings that are difficult to describe precisely in words, thereby enabling the audience to discover their own connections among memory, time, nature, and the world around them.

血食 / THE HEMATOPHAGY

← Back to workStatementKeyframesProcess
Project statement

A crane-like creature living within a human-made habitat suddenly pecks a zookeeper to death in an ordinary day. Then this creature runs away and disappears into a gap of reality.

The film explore ths differences between human and non-human cognitive systems, and how humans construct meaning through labeling and control. When these frameworks break down, so do the meanings and structures built upon them.

404共生系统
404 Symbiotic System

← Back to work Statement Process
Project statement

"404 Symbiotic System" is a series of paintings from a post-human perspective, focusing on new types of symbiotic relationships between humans, nature, and technology. It tries to express how human perception and understanding are constantly changing in an environment where ecology and technology intertwine, showing a kind of symbiosis that goes beyond traditional binary oppositions.

This is an exploration of the complex relationship between 'humans, nature, and technology.' In this context, '404' has been redefined from an error code into an untraceable node, symbolizing another fictional space. In this space, humans are no longer at the center and rebuild their understanding of themselves.

Exhibited in 2025 at the Luo Zhongli Art Museum, Chongqing, China, and subsequently participated in the “The Start of a Long Journey” Academy of Fine Arts National Exhibition Graduation Works.

← Back to work Statement Images
Project statement

ROTTEN ORANGE is a mixed-media animation about anxiety, combining hand-drawn, digital animation, and video. It combines the reasons and manifestations of people's anxiety from the survey with the artist's own feelings of anxiety.

This animation doesn't provide a solution for anxiety, just like the artist's state during the making of the animation — it simply shows the state and studies and reflects on it, without reaching a conclusion.
